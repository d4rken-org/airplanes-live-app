The official airplanes.live app for Android. Follow live air traffic from the airplanes.live community, get alerts when an aircraft, flight or squawk code shows up, and keep an eye on your own feeder.

Map
• Live aircraft with labels, tracks, details and photos
• Center on your position and see distances to aircraft

Search
• By callsign, registration, hex code, aircraft type, squawk or flight number
• Look around a place or near you
• See an airline's flights, or the flights at an airport

Watches and alerts
• Get a notification when a watched aircraft, flight, squawk code or area shows up
• Watches are checked in the background, also while the app is closed

Feeders
• Follow your feeder's message rates and history
• Get notified when a feeder goes offline or has a problem
• Add feeders by scanning their QR code

AR view
• Point your phone at the sky to see which aircraft are overhead

Free and Pro
Everything above works for free, with daily limits. Feeding airplanes.live? Link your feeder and the app goes Pro: higher limits, faster refreshes, more watches, aircraft history and theme options.

No sign-up, no ads, no tracking. Open source.
