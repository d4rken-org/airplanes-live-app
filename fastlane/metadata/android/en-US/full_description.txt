The official airplanes.live app for Android. See what's flying around you and anywhere in the world, live.

airplanes.live is a community of people who pick up aircraft signals with their own antennas and share them. Unlike some other flight trackers, the data is live, not delayed, and unfiltered. This app puts that network in your pocket.

✈️ Live map

Watch aircraft move in real time, with their callsigns, types and recent tracks. Tap one to see where it's flying from and to, what it is and who operates it. Follow it across the map, or look back at where it flew over the past week.

🏠 What flies over your home

Pick a spot and get notified when aircraft pass by. Later, check which aircraft flew over at any hour of the past week.

🔔 Alerts

Watch an aircraft, a flight or a squawk code and get a notification when it shows up, even while the app is closed.

🛫 Airports

See arrivals and departures with delays and cancellations, and follow flights on the map.

🔍 Search

Find aircraft by callsign, registration, type, squawk or flight number, or see what's flying near you or any other place.

🌧️ Weather and airspace

Show rain radar, airspace, flight restrictions and military activity on the map.

📱 AR view

Point your phone at the sky to see which aircraft you're looking at.

📡 For feeders

Keep an eye on your own feeder with its statistics, and get notified when it goes offline or has a problem.

This app has no ads, no analytics and doesn't sell your data. Some features require an upgrade.
