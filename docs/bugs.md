# Reporting problems
{: #reporting-problems }

## Ask a question
{: #questions }

Check the [FAQ](/faq) first. For questions and chat:

- [darken's Discord](https://discord.gg/ENtVkMHqZg): the app developer's community.
- [airplanes.live Discord](https://discord.gg/adsb): the official airplanes.live Discord, for questions about the network, coverage and feeding.

Both are also linked in **Settings** › **Support**.

## Report a bug
{: #report }

Open an issue on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/d4rken-org/airplanes-live-app/issues). Include:

- the app version (shown under **Changelog** in **Settings**) and where you installed it from,
- your phone model and Android version,
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead,
- a screenshot or screen recording if it helps.

Search the existing issues first; someone may have reported it already.

## Record a debug log
{: #debug-log }

A debug log records everything the app is doing, which often shows the cause of a problem.

1. Open **Settings** › **Support**, tap **Record debug log** and confirm.
2. Do what causes the problem.
3. Go back to **Settings** › **Support**, tap **Stop recording** and share the file.

The log may contain sensitive information, like your location or feeder ID. Only share it with people you trust. If you'd rather not attach it to a public issue, ask on darken's Discord where to send it.
