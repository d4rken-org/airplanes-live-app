The official airplanes.live app for Android.

Follow live air traffic from the airplanes.live community, get alerts when an aircraft, flight or squawk code shows up, and keep an eye on your own feeder.

Download User guide

Android 8.0+ · No ads · No tracking · Open source

What it does

  • A live map of the aircraft that airplanes.live receives, with labels, tracks, aircraft details and photos. Map
  • Search by callsign, registration, hex code, aircraft type, squawk or flight number, or look around a place. Search
  • Watch an aircraft, a flight, a squawk code or an area, and get a notification when it shows up. Watchlist & alerts
  • Follow your feeder's message rates and get notified when it goes offline. Feeders
  • Point your phone at the sky to see which aircraft are overhead in the AR view.
  • Feeding airplanes.live? Link your feeder and the app goes Pro. Free and Pro
